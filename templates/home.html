Under the Hood

Powered by Graph Algorithms

Every recommendation is derived from classical graph theory applied to a real athletic training dependency network.

⚡

Dijkstra's Algorithm

Finds the lowest-cost path from Desire to your goal event. Edge cost = weight × (node strength ÷ 100). Low strength means more room to improve — Dijkstra routes through the highest-leverage weaknesses.

🌐

BFS Traversal

Explores the full training graph level by level from Desire. Shows every training factor in dependency order — foundational habits first, race performance last.

📊

Degree Centrality

Ranks nodes by their number of connections scaled by current strength. High-centrality factors like Sleep and Consistency are load-bearing across every pathway in the graph.
